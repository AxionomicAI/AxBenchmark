# Suggested development sequence

This is a delivery recommendation derived from the module contracts, not an additional product requirement. The product requirements remain in [the source specification](../SPEC.md). Establish shared contracts before their producers and consumers; a contract being available does not mean its entire module is implemented. Keep every intermediate milestone explicitly partial until the final acceptance gate passes.

## Recommended order

| Step | Module | Why now and observable completion gate |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [M01 — Template library and identity](01-template-library-identity.md) | Establish the frozen template, revision, baseline, and deterministic identity contracts. Demonstrate identity stability across operating systems/repackaging and identity changes for benchmark-defining edits. |
| 2 | [M02 — Retained results and comparability](02-retained-results-comparability.md) | Establish result IDs, provenance, immutable original data, evidence references, and persisted lifecycle records before execution produces them. Round-trip representative complete, partial, imported, and ungraded records without mixing template identities. |
| 3 | [M03 — Environment readiness](03-environment-readiness.md) | Establish macOS/Linux capability and prerequisite reporting. Demonstrate that no-harness installations retain library, exchange, and saved-report access while planning/execution are blocked. |
| 4 | [M04 — Model catalog](04-model-catalog.md) | Resolve harness/provider/account capabilities and model/effort metadata before selecting models. Verify cache/override precedence, offline fallback, refresh failure, and unknown effort support. |
| 5 | [M05 — Harness execution and isolation](05-harness-execution-isolation.md) | Establish headless invocation, fresh task sessions, clean/current policies, independent workspaces, and process cleanup across supported harnesses. Verify requested versus effective settings and explicit isolation limitations. |
| 6 | [M06 — Scoring and rankings](06-scoring-rankings.md) | Establish weight validation, formulas, eligibility, judge groups, and missing/zero-cost rules before launch settings or presentation depend on them. Verify calculations from controlled result records without model calls. |
| 7 | [M07 — Run configuration](07-run-configuration.md) | Bind selections, judge, original weights, environment policy, and catalog provenance to a pinned template revision. Verify independent saved configurations, selection defaults, launch validation, and immutability after launch. |
| 8 | [M08 — Verification and evidence](08-verification-evidence.md) | Establish executable-check and evidence contracts before finalizing the built-in template. Verify disposable snapshots, separate process/check outcomes, browser dimensions, backend contracts, and infrastructure failures. |
| 9 | [M09 — Default inventory benchmark](09-default-inventory-benchmark.md) | Package the preserved specification and seven prompts with checks and the web rubric. Verify an empty baseline and planner-free reuse; preserve historical artifacts. |
| 10 | [M10 — Measurements and cost](10-measurements-cost.md) | Normalize execution events into durations, usage, cost bases, and coverage. Verify cumulative-event handling, phase timing exclusions, unknown costs, and recorded optional conversion rates. |
| 11 | [M11 — Run orchestration](11-run-orchestration.md) | Connect configurations, harness execution, verification, measurements, and persistence. Verify one concurrent configuration per harness, sequential queues/tasks, one-trial and timeout defaults, detach/reconnect, failure continuation, and explicit stop cleanup. |
| 12 | [M12 — Quality judging](12-quality-judging.md) | Review retained artifacts and verification evidence independently of execution metrics. Verify sequential fresh sessions, screenshot capability, strict grades, ungraded malformed reviews, and preservation of original reviews when rejudging. |
| 13 | [M13 — Standalone HTML report](13-standalone-html-report.md) | Deliver an inspectable comparison from retained records and scoring contracts. Verify offline direct-file opening, embedded evidence, required tables/charts, alternative weights, judge groups, and safe rendering. |
| 14 | [M14 — Command-line interface](14-command-line-interface.md) | Expose the complete unattended execution and inspection path; connect each command to its owning module as it becomes available. Validate full configuration, status/attach/stop behavior, and model-free report regeneration. Exchange commands reach final acceptance at step 17. |
| 15 | [M15 — Terminal interface](15-terminal-interface.md) | Build the primary end-to-end user workflow over established operations. Validate keyboard/mouse use, resizing, panels, logs, launch freeze, reconnect, and results exploration. Template-authoring and exchange actions reach final acceptance at steps 16–17. |
| 16 | [M16 — Custom template planning](16-custom-template-planning.md) | Extend the already runnable library with approval-driven planning, regeneration, and committed-repository capture. Exercise frontend, backend, and fullstack templates and prove later runs use the packaged snapshot. |
| 17 | [M17 — ZIP exchange](17-zip-exchange.md) | Add portable templates and results after identity, persistence, evidence, and consumer contracts are stable. Validate macOS/Linux exchange, bounded safe extraction, integrity checks, idempotency, conflicts, and atomic rejection; finish CLI/TUI exchange actions. |
| 18 | [M18 — Hardware monitoring](18-hardware-monitoring.md) | Add optional collectors to an already correct benchmark lifecycle. Integrate capability reporting, once-per-experiment sampling, retained samples, report timelines, and correct energy scope without changing core-run availability. |

## Integration checkpoints

1. **Foundation:** after steps 1–7, template identity, persistent records, capability decisions, launch choices, and score calculations have explicit, compatible contracts. No real benchmark completion is claimed yet.
2. **Measured execution:** after steps 8–11, the default seven-task template can execute with independent workspaces and retained checks/measurements. Verify ordinary failure versus authentication failure, interrupted execution, scheduling, and reconnect without task replay.
3. **Reviewed comparison:** after steps 12–15, finish the built-in-template journey through the TUI and unattended interface, with independent grading and a standalone report. Opening an exported HTML file offline and changing both weight sets must require no model calls.
4. **Full scope:** after steps 16–18, exercise custom frontend/backend/fullstack work, a committed-repository baseline, cross-machine ZIP exchange, imported judge groups, and optional monitoring. Complete every source acceptance criterion recorded in [the coverage ledger](TRACEABILITY.md).

Feature interfaces should be connected as each owner becomes available. The early Environment view contract includes unavailable monitoring; step 18 supplies optional telemetry rather than retroactively making sensors mandatory. Likewise, planner, competitor, and judge all reuse the headless/isolation contract, while retaining their different input and evidence restrictions.

## Final acceptance gate

Run the complete source acceptance suite on macOS and Linux, covering the no-harness and offline states, all supported harness integrations, simultaneous and sequential execution, terminal resizing/navigation, explicit stop cleanup, and frontend/backend/repository-baseline workflows. Validate same-template import across operating systems and ZIP repackaging, unsafe/corrupt/conflicting package rejection without partial additions, preservation of originals and history, and model-free report regeneration. Check numeric scores, incomplete measurements, verified zero costs, judge grouping, telemetry attribution, and direct-file reports with networking disabled. These are required verification scenarios, not claims that they have already passed.
