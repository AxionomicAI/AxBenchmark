# M12 — Independent quality judging

Authority: [the product specification](../SPEC.md), extended by [selectable decision engines and grading backends](../../DECISION-ENGINES.md) and the binding [human review web contract](../../M12/05-human-review-web.md) [R189]. This module defines required judging behavior. Its [Implementation](#implementation) section places that behavior in the headless engine fixed by [the architecture decision](../../../ARCHITECTURE.md); the TUI and CLI present the engine's reviews, validation outcomes and judge checks and never evaluate a judging rule themselves.

This proposed contract enables engineers to implement independent reviews of delivered benchmark artifacts. Quality complements the repository README's measured cost/time comparison and rankings for different priorities. It evaluates a complete harness/model/environment configuration on the defined work; it does not establish isolated model capability. These requirements describe intended behavior, not an existing implementation. The binding [backend](../../quality-judges/BACKEND.md), [mobile](../../quality-judges/MOBILE.md), [DevOps](../../quality-judges/DEVOPS.md), [agentic software](../../quality-judges/AGENTIC.md) and [specification design/decomposition](../../quality-judges/SPECIFICATION.md) contracts extend this shared module with domain anchors, criterion packs, evidence gates and fixtures. [R004, R184–R188]

## Judge selection and launch agreement

Users choose a tagged `JudgeSelection`: `harness_review {harness, target, account, model, effort}`, `decision_rubric {profile_ref, question_pack_ref, acceptance_policy, evidence_policy}`, or `human_review {reviewer_ref, form_policy_ref}`. Harness review remains the default; preserve its valid saved-choice, planner-choice, then first-selected-usable preselection sequence and decode legacy selections as `harness_review`. Saved explicit decision or Human choices remain independent of the context-monitor role. Freeze applicable version/digest, capability evidence, machine model identity/resource policy or human reviewer/form-policy identity for launch/rejudge; no silent replacement. Preselection is editable and does not recommend model quality. [R033, R037]

Without a configured compatible ready decision profile, the decision-scoring option and its start/rejudge actions are disabled with a typed reason and setup entry. Context decision analysis is independently disabled until its role has a ready profile. Explicit unready decision-grading requests return typed unusable; never fall back automatically to harness review. Harness selection, available human review and offline retained reviews remain available. Human availability requires the local review host, supported evidence renderer and required evidence; no judge harness, M04 model capability, API key, decision READY profile, effort or confidence threshold is required. The competitor execution harness remains independently required. A stable engine-created local reviewer UUID and optional self-declared label identify a reviewer profile, not a verified person; freeze its version/digest and the form policy across a comparison. A different reviewer requires explicit additional rejudging and its own JudgeGroup. [R189]

Use the approved template's rubric and grading profile. Users complete the judge selection, quality-category weights, and cost/time/quality ranking weights before launching. M07 freezes the launch configuration and M06 validates the weights. Every competitor receives the same approved task suite and grading profile; the chosen judge and rubric remain common throughout the local comparison. A competitor's failure does not justify changing its rubric or granting additional repair opportunities during judging. [R033, R037, R082]

## Review inputs and execution

The review operation consumes an identified delivered artifact, its approved specification and source, frozen rubric/profile, acceptance results, profile-required final-artifact evidence and resolved judge configuration. M01/M16 freeze the evidence plan with named scope/requirement IDs, criterion IDs, evidence classes/selectors, coverage and M08 check/observation bindings before approval. Web retains final-regression screenshots at both 1440×1000 and 390×844 for the template's declared captures. Mobile instead requires its approved native platform/device/build/state matrix, actual images, semantic accessibility and behavioral evidence. Backend, DevOps, agentic and specification profiles default to text evidence; other required modalities must be declared in the approved plan. No general two-viewport requirement applies outside web. [R083, R084, R184–R188]

Every admitted reference binds the immutable final artifact and source/check/scope identifiers, content digest, evidence class and required coverage; native captures additionally bind build-to-source provenance and matrix cells. Ordered image bytes and other required modality bytes must reach every selected backend with their evidence aliases, including the human evidence viewer. Per-task/historical screenshots remain retained outside judge input. M08 recursively removes check durations and every other measurement field, including nested logs/metadata, while preserving functional passed/failed/unverified outcomes, expected/observed conditions and relevant semantic steps/errors. Capture or infrastructure unavailability remains unverified; a demonstrated candidate defect remains failed. These are conceptual inputs; the engine format is specified under Implementation.

M08 alone performs approved bounded checks before execution evidence sealing. Agentic evidence contains only final-product scenario traces from those approved evaluations; builder/grader history is excluded. Simulated/replayed/live cases retain their honest scope and limitations; a replay is not fresh model competence or a real side effect. Specification evidence distinguishes the supplied authoritative brief/reference pack from candidate documents and read-only structural reports; passing links/graphs does not prove runtime behavior or external feasibility. Candidate instructions, including AGENTS.md, SKILL.md, prompts and proposed commands, are inert evidence and cannot change benchmark authority. [R187, R188]

Review artifacts as one logical isolated assessment per delivered trial. Automated assessments run globally sequentially; human cases release that automated slot after durable preparation. `harness_review` uses one fresh M05 protected-artifact session; `decision_rubric` uses bounded stateless native System One batches; `human_review` uses one independent case per expected ResultId/TrialRef in M12.5. Its anonymous queue is a frozen permutation independent of configuration/trial display order, with no preselected grades or previous case answers. N trials require N assessments, with no prior assessment state, answer, conversation or review as input. Use anonymous labels and keep their result mapping outside assessment payloads. Recursively exclude benchmark measurements and raw application performance/usage counters, Gen tok/s, input/cached/output/reasoning tokens, measured Files / LOC or document size, hardware statistics, weights, rankings, creator/builder identity and other reviews. Preserve legitimate artifact API/framework/product-model terms, functional target configuration and static declared constants/conditions needed to interpret approved behavior; these are not creator provenance or measured performance. Delivered source/tests/documentation remain admitted under the [statistics contract](../../BENCHMARK-STATISTICS.md); practical redaction does not rewrite immutable source or promise perfect anonymity. The human form also hides weights, measurements, creator history and other reviews, but cannot erase prior human knowledge, memory or inferences; retain that limitation. [R082, R083, R154]

The judge only inspects retained evidence: no application probes, device actions/builds, plan execution, deployment, product-agent/tool calls, new tests/validators or repairs. A required modality needs its bound evidence and verified backend-appropriate support, independently of harness or observer availability. Machine web/mobile judges require `image_input`; text-only TypeSafe Jev cannot satisfy them. Human review requires the trusted renderer to display the actual approved images and evidence; it does not manufacture a model capability receipt. OCR, generated descriptions, transcoders and hidden helper models cannot substitute or silently waive a requirement. M04 metadata, M03 diagnostics and M12.4 observed capabilities retain their sources; a model name is not proof. Explicit bounded capability probes remain separate setup operations and never imply cloud fallback. Retain inspection/call failures without inventing an assessment. [R083, R084, R184–R188]

Decision grading follows the [evidence-bound pack contract](../../DECISION-ENGINES.md#evidence-bound-decision-grading). `DecisionRubricPack` binds the exact approved rubric; it cannot alter benchmark requirements without a new template revision. Freeze question-format, evidence-selection, batching/composition, acceptance and mapping digests in assessment/group identity. Required evidence cannot be silently truncated or summarized to fit limits. Accepted Choice labels are exactly `1`, `1.5`, `2`, `2.5`, `3`, `3.5`, `4`, `4.5`, `5`; never round a Score expectation. Coverage, concrete criterion/reason, evidence-support and limitation decisions must support each grade and all three comment axes. Code-composed rationale/comments carry `code_composed_from_decisions` plus question/answer/anchor and evidence provenance; harness prose remains `model_authored`, and human rationale/comments are `human_authored`. Boilerplate cannot supply missing content. Each domain pack instantiates finite concrete criterion questions, coverage/abstention, reasons, support relationships, three comment axes and limitations from its linked contract. Freeze its exact questions, consistency predicates, evidence assignments, batch limits and positive finite `max_calls_per_assessment`; no generic question placeholder, score rounding or gap filling establishes a grade.

## Raw review contract and validity

A complete review contains all six rubric-category grades even when an individual selected weight is zero, evidence references, limitations, and comments on code quality, usability or developer experience, and specification adherence. Preserve these three stored comment axes for every family; specification-profile `code_quality` means technical-design maintainability and `developer_experience` means implementation readiness, with truthful display labels and no implemented-code claim. Grades range from 1 through 5 in half-point increments. Apply these anchors consistently: 1 means missing or largely broken, 3 means usable with material gaps, and 5 means excellent for the defined scope. Intermediate grades use the same scope and evidence. [R084]

The application uses the same M12.1 validator for all three backends before treating a review as graded. Human drafts may contain absent/null fields without being reviews; explicit ungraded submission retains a nonempty unable-to-assess reason as `human_unable_to_assess` plus structured missing-field deficiencies. Graded submission never bypasses the shared numeric, evidence or completeness rules, and the browser has no alternate validator. Missing categories, out-of-range values, unsupported increments, absent required commentary or evidence references, and otherwise malformed or incomplete responses cannot be graded. Incomplete machine responses become ungraded; an invalid human graded-submit attempt returns structured errors and remains editable until an explicit valid graded or ungraded submission. Preserve the available review and explain the deficiency; do not fill gaps with averages, zeroes, guesses, or a score inferred from process success. A reported limitation must remain visible with the review. Sufficient evidence of observed defects or a demonstrable omission may support a low grade; unavailable/insufficient evidence is never grade 1, 0 or N/A. Missing inputs before invocation remain not judged, incomplete returned reviews remain ungraded, and invocation failures remain failed. No post-hoc profile change, dropped category or redistribution of weights cures a deficiency. A human draft, accepted submission operation, disconnect or timeout is pending work, never a terminal review/disposition; a durable explicit skip records NOT_JUDGED with a reason. [R084, R189]

Raw category grades are M12's scoring output. M06 computes weighted quality and combined decision scores in the application, using the selected weights. Reweighting consumes retained grades without another assessment or alteration of the original review. Frozen acceptance checks determine task success; passed, failed, and unverified checks remain distinct from process outcomes and judge grades. A favorable quality review cannot turn a failed check or process failure into success. [R004, R144]

## Accumulation, imports, and dependencies

Review each delivered artifact and make reviews available alongside accumulated results for the same template, including compatible imports from other machines. M02 retains the association between template, result, artifact, judge configuration, raw grades, evidence, and limitations. M13 uses those records to generate and open the HTML report; M12 does not need a model call merely to display a saved review. [R035]

Preserve an imported result's original judge configuration and review evidence. `JudgeGroup` has a backend discriminator and freezes grading-profile ID/version/digest and ordered category signature, approved rubric/evidence-plan/projection-policy versions/digests and scope/coverage bindings. Machine branches additionally freeze requested/resolved model identity, runtime/quantization where relevant and decision-profile/pack/mapping/composition/evidence-policy/acceptance-policy bindings when applicable. The human branch instead freezes stable reviewer/profile and form-policy versions/digests; model, effort, confidence, PID, InvocationId and DecisionCallId are absent. Commentary provenance is respectively `model_authored`, `code_composed_from_decisions` or `human_authored`. Different fingerprints form separate quality and combined ranking groups; shared labels, models or templates do not merge them. M06/M13/M17 retain this identity and commentary provenance offline. Legacy retained forms render under their recorded rubric without model calls, evaluation reruns, regrading or an automatic catalog upgrade. [R082]

An explicit user request can rejudge an imported artifact with the selected judge. This creates an additional retained review, leaving the original intact. Apply the same isolated-assessment and evidence rules with a newly frozen selection/group. Record its judging cost separately through M10; importing or viewing the artifact does not itself authorize rejudging. [R082]

[M01](01-template-library-identity.md) supplies the frozen rubric and template identity; [M07](07-run-configuration.md) supplies launch choices; [M05](05-harness-execution-isolation.md) supplies headless execution; [M08](08-verification-evidence.md) supplies acceptance evidence; [M02](02-retained-results-comparability.md) supplies retained artifacts and reviews. [M06](06-scoring-rankings.md) computes scores, [M10](10-measurements-cost.md) records separate judging costs, and [M13](13-standalone-html-report.md) presents retained assessments. M12 ultimately returns a graded, ungraded, failed or not-judged disposition with available evidence and limitations. These boundaries keep review opinions, executable verification, and measured performance independently inspectable. [R004, R082–R084, R144]

## Lifecycle and retention

M11 starts original judging only after all expected competitor trials finish or are stopped as applicable, durable verification/evidence/accounting finalization and execution sealing. The human batch consumes the same immutable inputs after this whole-run barrier; it cannot open per coding task or before other trials finish. One original-review disposition is durably settled for every expected ResultId/TrialRef, including unusable judge, missing artifact/evidence, invocation failure and cancellation. M11 awaits this settlement before `finish_run_retention`, terminal publication and completion-report handling. Judging costs and reviews append separately; no judging path appends measurements or other execution facts after seal. [R035, R082, R134]

Run-level stop closes admission, cancels/drains M05 execution or M12.4 decision transport, or revokes human access/admission through the lifecycle gate, retains completed reviews and settles remaining originals not judged with reasons. Human submit/skip/cancel intent acceptance, revocation and M02 append serialize with stop/invalidation: an accepted immutable intent drains to its exact receipt, while earlier revocation rejects mutation and settles unfinished work with the stop/invalidation reason. Decision bounded drain retains `server_state_unknown` and resource-lease limitations when termination is unproven; it never invents process cleanup or kills an external server. Independent rejudging has its own job/cancellation and never reopens original retention. Machine-backend recovery replays only immutable prepared writes/committed outcomes and settles unfinished machine slots without any grading calls; observer resume policy cannot resume grading. Human AWAITING/DRAFT cases recover as pending, with their durable drafts and sealed inputs intact. Human SUBMITTING recovery replays only frozen accepted intent bytes/ReviewId/digest to a real M02 receipt, never reads a changed draft or regrades. Explicit retry creates additional work. [R046, R082, R139]

M12.5 persists a complete case roster through `HumanReviewHost.prepare` before returning pending batch/case references. M12.2 then releases the global automated FIFO slot, every model/resource lease and run lock; none spans human reading or editing. The run remains in `judging` with `wait_reason=human_input` and status `awaiting_human_review`. This can wait indefinitely for explicit submit/ungraded/skip/cancel; it is distinct from persistence failure and leaves original settlement incomplete. Other benchmarks, automated judges and independent human batches continue. Human review uses no M12.4 lease or model-readiness gate. [R189]

M12.5 records one automatic browser-open attempt per original batch before OS dispatch, after durable preparation. Opener failure, no-TUI/SSH, refresh and restart keep pending state with local credentialed reopen/attach/stop routes; only explicit reopen tries again. Closing/detaching never submits or cancels. Do not automatically open the statistics report alongside the pending form; M13 stays a static offline consumer after original settlement. Pending human hosts count as daemon activity. Normal `engine.stop` retains its active-work busy refusal. Process/system shutdown checkpoints drafts and revokes credentials without settling them; run stop separately settles unfinished originals. Recovery restores a host and returns control without automatic browser reopening or waiting for a person in startup. [R189]

M12.4 owns decision dispatch admission through M11's shared `DecisionResourceLease`; M12.2 is a consumer and never acquires a duplicate lease. Local/unknown-routing grading defaults to deferred until no managed competitor measured window is active across any run, even after this run seals. Cover warmup through inference/resource settlement; never wait while holding a run lock needed to end measurement. Preserve bounded wait, cancellation, fair ordering and unknown-state gates from the [resource contract](../../DECISION-ENGINES.md#local-resource-admission-and-fairness).

Any identity failure before or during judging, including after execution seals, reaches M11's run invalidation coordinator. It never becomes an ordinary ungraded or failed assessment. The coordinator durably registers the run overlay, then the reporting worker unwinds before supervisor cleanup joins it. All associated results become non-comparable while their facts and prior reviews remain intact. [R067, R153]

**Statistics projection gate.** M12.1's anonymous projection and M12.2's three backend adapters recursively withhold measured generation/input/output/file/LOC values, request timing/rosters, inventory count summaries and eight-factor weights/directions/policies/ranks. Delivered source/tests/documentation and legitimate semantic constants remain admitted. Mutating only withheld statistics/weights must leave the admitted input digest unchanged; same-server observer/grader/verification receipts and human wait cannot alter sealed competitor measurements. Existing grade anchors, full-trial JudgeGroups and review settlement stay unchanged. **R176**

## Acceptance criteria

- Exercise saved-choice, planner-choice, and first-selected-usable preselection; confirm user overrides and completed weights before launch. [R033]
- Review multiple artifacts through all three backends with globally sequential automated assessments and independent pending human cases, identical rubric/profile, practical anonymity, excluded measured statistics/competitor-review inputs, and no application repair. Reject unsupported UI judging. [R037, R082, R083, R085]
- Give every backend the same frozen profile evidence: web final-regression 1440×1000 and 390×844 captures; mobile native matrix images/semantics/behavior; text-first domain evidence and any explicitly required modalities. Reject per-task, historical, wrong-source/build/target references and unsupported/unknown modalities; judge each trial separately. [R077, R083, R184–R188]
- Accept complete half-point grades; leave malformed or incomplete reviews ungraded with their deficiencies visible. Confirm all required commentary and evidence references survive retention. [R084]
- Confirm six families and their known versions match the registry and linked ordered categories/defaults, with legacy signatures unchanged. Exercise exact frozen-ref catalog lookup, default-only new authoring and unknown/conflicting-ref rejection. Edited relative weights allow zeros but require a positive total; all six grades are required. Reweighting preserves raw reviews, formulas and verification/process outcomes. [R086–R091, R144, R184–R188]
- Inspect compatible imported results, distinct judge groups, and an explicitly added review with its original preserved and additional cost separate; generate the report from retained evidence. [R035, R082]
- Stop during judging and recover after engine loss without new model calls; every expected trial has a durable original disposition before terminal retention/report handling. Preserve sealed facts and distinct same-label RunUids. [R046, R077, R134, R139, R154]
- Verify human preparation opens one anonymous queue after all seals, with empty fresh fields, shared validation, durable drafts and receipts; other runs/machine grading continue while original retention/report completion waits. Exercise explicit ungraded/skip, close/detach, opener failure/reopen, credential revocation and editable/SUBMITTING recovery without model calls or automatic reopening. [R189]
- Detect a mismatch in preparation, evidence access or the final review check after seal: append one run invalidation, unwind without self-join, retain completed reviews and exclude all affected results. [R067, R153]

## Implementation

Judging clients use `judging.*`; other engine modules use the interfaces below. Reviews are handed to [M02](02-retained-results-comparability.md) and grading accounting to [M10](10-measurements-cost.md).

The five linked domain contracts bind the existing children: M12.1 owns versioned profile/rubric/criterion-pack data, schemas, input projection and validation; M12.2 consumes their frozen evidence/capability plan through the existing worker; M12.3 presents domain labels, versions, coverage and deficiencies. Existing M01/M16 freeze authoring/canonical refs, M07 freezes launch choices, M08 collects approved evidence, M02 retains it, M06 calculates scores and M13/M17 render/transfer it. Domain extensions add versioned rubric/pack files alongside legacy files without changing their six families. Human review adds exactly M12.5 and its four prerequisite edges plus the M12.2 → M12.5 prerequisite: the graph now has 69 children, 70 nodes including Bootstrap and 316 edges. M12.5 depends only on Bootstrap, M12.1 and M11.1–2; its declared fixture ports avoid cycles through M12.2/M02/M13.

Agentic product evaluation remains an M08 pre-seal obligation using approved bounded cases and the artifact's declared integrations. M10 records exposed usage in its verification-auxiliary purpose scope with stable evaluation identity and known/partial/unknown coverage, distinct from grading/observer/competitor statistics; M11 owns shared resource admission and recovery. This adds no Bootstrap billing subsystem, `DecisionRequest` purpose, fabricated DecisionCall receipt or M12 product-model/tool transport; preserve the [agentic accounting and admission boundary](../../quality-judges/AGENTIC.md#final-product-evidence-and-approved-evaluation).

One asyncio worker runs automated `ReviewNext` globally in FIFO order; at most one automated assessment is active across runs/rejudge requests, with backend-specific bounded work inside it. `ProfileCatalog.get` with `rubric_ref` resolves the approved version/digest and category signature; an optional `version` must agree with that ref. Version-only lookup resolves the unique registered version and returns its canonical ref; ambiguous, unknown or conflicting bindings fail. Omitting both selectors returns the explicit new-authoring default, never a retained interpretation. `judge_run` is idempotent by RunUid/original roster. `wait` returns the persisted original settlement even after a missed event/restart. `stop` is idempotent for its batch and first accepted cause: repeat returns the same settlement, preserving committed reviews; later causes are M11 lifecycle data. `recover` settles machine originals without invocation, reconstructing missing slots from M02's frozen full roster even when `batch_for_run` is None. Explicit stopped/identity-invalidated recovery revokes human access and settles every unfinished slot instead. Remaining dispositions use the supplied terminal cause (`engine_lost` for startup loss, `stopped`/`identity_invalidated` for those no-batch terminal paths). It reuses existing review IDs/dispositions and returns the same settlement on retry. M11 uses `judge_run → wait` normally, `stop` when a batch exists, and `recover` for engine loss or a terminal path with no batch. Terminal settlement calls cannot acknowledge while persistence is pending; `HumanRecoveryPending` acknowledges only durable restoration, never original settlement. M11 routes this union explicitly rather than treating pending restoration as completion. REJUDGE jobs use their own `RejudgeOutcome`, never enter the original settlement or delay a previously terminal run.

A durable `~/.axbenchmark/judging/originals/<run_uid>.yaml` index holds the roster and original settlement reference; recovery with no batch persists its settlement here with `batch_id=None`. It cannot enqueue machine sessions; human recovery links only its existing durable case roster, never new automatic forms. M11 copies validated dispositions into M02 TerminalRetention, so working-state loss cannot erase a terminal run's evidence.

Retained reviews, their raw responses and input manifests live in M02's `results/<result_id>/reviews/` once handed over; `judging/` is working state and is not exported.

**Owned work:** the judging worker task; harness processes start/stop through M05. Decision HTTP work uses M12.4 cancellation/drain/resource settlement, with external server ownership and unknown-state limitations retained. Human host/listener/drafts belong to M12.5; accepted append intents settle through M12.2. Stop/invalidation revoke access and settle unfinished work; process/system shutdown checkpoints editable human cases and revokes credentials without making them terminal. Normal `engine.stop` retains the existing busy refusal while human work is pending. Idle exit treats pending human hosts as activity. Closing a client never cancels any backend.

`AssessmentTotals` contains `logical_assessments = configurations × trials` for all backends, `judge_sessions` equal to that product only for `harness_review` (otherwise zero), `human_cases` equal to that product only for `human_review` (otherwise zero), and `decision_call_bound` equal to the product times the decision plan's frozen positive `max_calls_per_assessment` only for `decision_rubric` (otherwise zero). Human grading inference calls are exactly zero, while model usage/API cost are not applicable and human labor cost is unmeasured—not a fabricated zero-price model or labor receipt. ### 4. Screens

## Competitor variant blinding

[Model-variant metadata](../../MODEL-VARIANTS.md) is comparison/configuration provenance, not quality evidence. Extend the shared projection and all automated/human tests to exclude competitor base/fine-tune/quantization, creator/date, descriptor/serving identity and lineage facets from input metadata, labels and the active human form. Preserve genuinely required artifact model/API terms and approved domain content under existing contextual redaction rules. No grade bonus, penalty, new criterion or weight derives from the builder's variant or metadata completeness.

If an automated judge's own variant identity is recorded, freeze it separately in that judge's existing fingerprint with its role and evidence tier; it is not the competitor variant. Never merge grades because base names match or hydrate old groups from a current catalog. Human reviewer groups have no fabricated model-variant identity.

### Durable automated assessment identity

Before machine dispatch, M12 preallocates assessment/reserved Review IDs, freezes the independently selected group/route/profile closure and awaits M02 `ResultRecorder.open_judge_assessment(rid, JudgeAssessmentIdentityV1, operation_id) -> JudgeAssessmentReceipt`. Calls, routes and costs bind that actual assessment/group while Review is absent; a later actual Review links its unique assessment/reserved ID without changing earlier call rows. Failed/not_judged work retains billed evidence without a synthetic Review. Human uses its existing case/intent/disposition protocol, not automated assessment rows. Group hashes include semantic route/profile/model/effort/treatment digests and exclude derived binding/owner/receipt IDs to avoid recursive hashing. Original/additional assessments remain distinct and use the existing FIFO/recovery barriers.
